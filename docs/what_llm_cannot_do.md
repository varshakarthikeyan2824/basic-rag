# What an LLM Cannot Do

## Introduction

A Large Language Model (LLM) is an AI model that understands and generates human-like text based on patterns learned during training. LLMs are useful for answering questions, summarizing documents, generating code and assisting with everyday tasks. However, they have limitations that users should understand.

## 1. Guarantee that every answer is correct

An LLM can generate incorrect or misleading information, even when the answer sounds confident. This is commonly known as hallucination. Important information should always be verified.

## 2. Know everything about the present

An LLM may not know about recent events, updated company policies or new information unless it has access to suitable external data or tools.

## 3. Think and understand exactly like a human

An LLM identifies patterns in data and generates responses. It does not have human experiences, personal understanding or common sense in the same way people do.

## 4. Access private information automatically

An LLM cannot automatically access private company documents, databases or customer records. It needs authorized data sources and integrations to retrieve that information.

## 5. Guarantee unbiased responses

LLMs learn from training data, which may contain biases. As a result, their responses can sometimes reflect those biases.

## 6. Guarantee security and privacy

An LLM alone cannot guarantee that sensitive information is protected. Organizations must use appropriate access controls, secure integrations and data-handling policies.

## 7. Replace human judgment completely

LLMs can assist with decision-making, but they should not independently make important business, financial, legal or safety decisions without appropriate human review.

## Conclusion

LLMs are powerful tools that can improve productivity and automate many tasks. However, they are not perfect or fully independent systems. Organizations should combine LLM capabilities with reliable data, proper testing, security controls and human supervision to use AI responsibly.
